# VANIKARA V2 — Vanikara Console Admin User Guide

## 1. Accessing Vanikara Console
- Navigate to `/admin` (or `/admin/login`).
- Sign in with your provisioned staff username and password. Public Google accounts cannot access Vanikara Console.
- Sessions remain active for 8 hours and can be ended immediately via **Sign out** in the bottom-left sidebar.

## 2. Console Modules
1. **Command Centre**: Live database-derived CRM metrics, conversion summaries, and recent leads.
2. **Website Studio**: Edit live website copy, leadership profiles, timeline milestones, products, and legal facts. Clicking **Save** immediately purges the site cache so changes publish without redeployment.
3. **CRM & Enquiries**: Review contact form submissions, inspect details, update statuses, and archive resolved enquiries.
4. **Recruitment**:
   - **Job Positions**: Create, publish, close, or archive genuine open roles displayed on `/careers`.
   - **Applications**: Review candidate submissions, update recruitment statuses (`submitted`, `under_review`, `shortlisted`, `interview_scheduled`, `selected`, `not_selected`, etc.), and download résumés via temporary 1-hour signed links.
5. **Products & Interest**: Manage early interest registrations from restaurants, delivery partners, customers, and business partners submitted on `/food-delivery#register`.
6. **User Management**: View public Google-signed-in accounts and block or unblock abusive accounts (blocks take effect immediately on server requests).
7. **Security & Audit**: Inspect your active RBAC role, session expiration, configuration notices, and the immutable administrative audit log.
8. **Privacy Centre**: Review cookie consent statistics and update consent policy text.
9. **System Health**: Verify live database table connectivity and service status.
